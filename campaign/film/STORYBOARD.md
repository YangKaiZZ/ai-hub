# AI Hub - "One intelligent hub" (v1)

## Decisions

- **Story:** a student drowning in notifications has an idea, opens AI Hub, and each feature takes one weight off.
  It ends on the lockup with the student waving. The hero is unnamed; the viewer is meant to see themselves in him.
- **Format:** 1920x1080, 55s, no voiceover. Original lo-fi bed at 96 BPM (beat 0.625s, bar 2.5s), synthesized with SFX.
  Tense, filtered intro (bars 0-2), a breath for the idea (bars 3-4, riser), then the groove drops on the logo
  reveal (bar 5) and holds through the features. One Cmaj9 chord rings under the lockup.
- **Spine:** the die-cut sticker. Every scene has the student as a sticker reacting to the UI, and every UI panel
  lands with the same spring as a sticker slapped onto a laptop.
- **Camera:** one CSS-3D rig per scene, perspective 1866px; constant drift; parallax between stickers, UI and dots.
- **Brand:** AI Hub tokens only (violet `#7c3aed`, ink `#17162a`, paper `#f6f5fb`), Geist 500/700/800, real mark.
- **Bans:** emoji, neon glow, purple-to-blue gradients, colored left-border cards, spinners (shimmer instead), no "Matcha".
- **Seams:** whip pans between feature scenes (ground-only frames), push-through solids into and out of the brand
  moments (violet `#7c3aed`), one card plate into the dashboard.

## Locked

- 2026-10-06, user: delegated every creative call ("you be the creative director ... you do everything"). Built as v1.
  Times below are SCENE-LOCAL seconds; beat n = n x 0.625s.

## Frame 1 - Overload (s01-chaos, 3 bars, 7.5s)

Notification cards for seven deadlines and a meeting pop in one per beat (b0-b7) around the frame at different depths,
with pings and phone buzzes. The facepalm sticker slams in at b4. Kinetic type on b8, b9, b10: "7 deadlines." /
"4 courses." / "1 tired brain." Whip pan out to the left from b11. Seam out: ground only.

## Frame 2 - The idea (s02-idea, 2 bars, 5s)

Whip in: the thinking sticker (with the lightbulb) lands on b1. The bulb lights on b2 (ding, warm halo). The type
"What if it all" / "lived in *one place?*" builds on b3-b5. From b6 the camera dives into the bulb while a violet
disc grows to cover the frame. Seam out: full-frame `#7c3aed`.

## Frame 3 - Reveal (s03-reveal, 2 bars, 5s)

Opens on solid violet, which irises down into the AI Hub mark as the groove drops on frame 0 (impact). The wordmark
slides out on b1 (chime), and the tagline "Your entire student life. / One intelligent hub." follows on b3. The hero
sticker (AI Hub on his laptop) pops up on b4. From b6 a white card grows out of his laptop screen to the card plate.
Seam out: card plate 1440x810 at (240,135), radius 28, white.

## Frame 4 - Smart priorities (s04-priorities, 3 bars, 7.5s)

Card plate becomes the dashboard ("Welcome back", stats Due 3 / High 1 / Upcoming 6 / Done 12, counting on b1-b2).
On b4 a shimmer sweeps and "AI analyzed 5 tasks" pops. Tasks re-sort on b5 and b6 so the ERD project rises to the top,
and priority badges stamp on b7. The recommendation slides in on b8 and the arms-crossed sticker leans in. The label
"Knows what's due first" lands on b9. Whip out on b11.

## Frame 5 - AI Tutor (s05-tutor, 3 bars, 7.5s)

Whip in: the Tutor panel with the student sitting on a crate, on his phone. "Learning - hints first" is selected on
b1 (click). The question "How do I turn this many-to-many into tables?" types in from b2 to b4, then sends on b5.
The tutor's hint streams in from b6. The label "A tutor, not a shortcut." lands on b8. Whip out on b11.

## Frame 6 - Answers with sources (s06-sources, 2 bars, 5s)

## Frame 7 - AI Plan My Week (s07-plan, 2 bars, 5s)

## Frame 8 - Grades + LMS sync (s08-grades, 2 bars, 5s)

Seam out: full-frame `#7c3aed`.

## Frame 9 - Finale (s09-finale, 3 bars, 7.5s)

Violet irises open onto the celebrating sticker as confetti bursts on b1. "Same you." lands on b2 and
"Bigger goals." on b3. On b6 the scene glides to the lockup: the mark, "AI Hub", "Your entire student life. One
intelligent hub.", and "Free to start · Works with any LMS", with the waving sticker popping in on b8. The lockup
holds from 5.5s to the end.
